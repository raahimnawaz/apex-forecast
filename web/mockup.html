Glass here is real refraction where the browser allows it: a displacement map is generated per panel from a squircle lens profile and Snell's law, then fed to an SVG feDisplacementMap used as a backdrop-filter. Only Chromium supports that, so elsewhere the panel degrades to frosted blur and still reads as glass. Substrate tiles lift ~2% off the page with a single hairline — no blur, no shine. Metal is trim only: the lit top bevel, the rail, the tick marks.
